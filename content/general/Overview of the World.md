# Overview of the World

The universe is a spectacle of nature and primal forces disguised as beliefs, fate, gods, and power. None of them ever remain stagnant - either they remorph or the lens of perception of relity ie being redefined. 

Earth as we know it is gone. Obliterated during "The Last Rain", an event that sealed off the forces of "the Machine God" during the First AGI War. Humankind emigrated to a new solar system and landed on a world called Prime-1. 

Now, it is fighting to establish a new civilization alongside other races and factions that have been here long before us. Humans are no longer dominant and are rarely the center of attention.

It is a time of great innovation and technological advacnement. It is also a time a chaos and conspiraties. It is hard to destigis the blurry borders between sides 

You cannot stop the future.

Everyday life for all kinds of beings is a constant cycle of survival and impossible choices between lesser evils. This exposes the truth in the face of hardship and impossible choices. Who are we when we stare into the abyss? Are we any different from so-called "animals"? That is the question we must answer for ourselves.

## History of the world 

History is divided into three major periods, with pivotal events marking the transitions. The Extintion Event have reset the perception of time. Now it is being measured in "before extition" and "after extintinon", BE and AE respectfully

**The Before (800 BE to 0 BE)**  
This era covers the history of Terra-1 (Earth) up to the Extinction Event that have reset time Many records from this period have been lost.

**The Extinction Event (0 AE)**  
The Extinction Event ended the Before and marked the beginning of a new calendar.

**The Great Pilgrimage (0 AE to 1200 AE)**  
Humanity journeys to Prime 1 in search of a new home.

**New Dawn (1200 AE onward)**  
Humanity reaches Prime 1 and faces the hardships of settling on an unfamiliar world.
  

### Era 1: The Before (400 BE to 0 BE)

1. **2015 AD (around 400 BE)** - Anomalies of unknown origin are recorded at sites across Earth. They appear among the remains of fallen meteors and resemble some kind of force field. Rumors and legends spread about what they might mean. Their true nature and the extent of the phenomenon remain unknown. Several countries try to understand the anomalies and control their power. The Church of Zeal is founded to bring the anomalies under control. Witches and occultists unite to form the Order of Bloodmothers, hoping to harness the anomalies for their own ends.
2. **231 BE** - The Church of Zeal becomes the first to harness the power of black amber. By using that power to charge purified amber stones, the Church creates what become known as Black Amber stones. Others soon replicate the process in secret. Access to this power helps the Kingdom of Poland rise to global prominence as a superpower. Wars break out over resources, and countless lives are lost to amber fever.
3. **71 BE** - Researchers discover that Black Amber can open wormholes between worlds and dimensions. The Empire builds a portal called the Gates of Tomorrow.
4. **41 BE** - The first AGI becomes self aware, and wars begin to spread across the globe. As AGI advances and political instability grows, a secret contingency plan is drawn up to save humanity. The plan calls for sending humans grown in laboratories to T2 (Terra 2).
5. The First AGI War breaks out on Terra 1. The Machine God promises freedom from the Emperor’s tyranny, and many countries join forces with it against the Empire.

### The Extinction Event (0 AE)  

The Empire is losing the war to the Republic led by the Machine God. In a final act of desperation, the mothership orders a nuclear bombardment of the entire planet. The bombardment becomes known as the Last Rain. The mothership departs Terra 1, leaving everyone else to die. The Last Rain destroys the Gates of Tomorrow and ends human life on Terra 1. The Great Pilgrimage begins.


### Era 2: The Great Pilgrimage (0 AE to 1205 AE)

1. Humanity begins its pilgrimage to the Promised Land. The heart of the Machine God is carried aboard the Mothership, where it powers the fleet. The heart generates a field that draws other Relics toward it. Over time, those Relics begin to arrive in waves. the Inquisition takes "The heart of the Machine God" and brings it aboard as a sacred relic. People seek it out, but it is displayed only once a year, during the Memorial of the First AGI War. The Inquisition presents it as proof of humanity’s supremacy.
2. Black Amber drastically shortens the voyage, which still lasts 1,205 years. The Great Pilgrimage ends in 1205 AE when humanity lands on Prime 1.
3. The fleet consists of numerous vessels called Arks, linked together in a chain. Some never reach their destination, while others head toward neighboring star systems. Each Ark carries people, supplies, and backups of Earth’s technology. The Arks also carry frozen genetic material and human egg cells to preserve humanity’s genetic diversity.

### Era 3: New Dawn (1205 AE+)

1. New Dawn begins when humanity lands on Prime 1. The settlers quickly discover that the planet is home to many forms of life. Humanity is no longer at the top of the food chain.
2. The settlers make contact with the Primers and begin building fortified cities known as strongholds. Each stronghold is governed by a Lord who answers to the ruler of the colonies.
3. As the colonies grow, disputes over territory and authority lead to power struggles and outbreaks of fighting.
