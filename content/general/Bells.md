# Bells

> "Hope you will life to hear another bell." - Gate Guard (propably)

Sound vibrations play a vital role in the natural world of Prime 1. We still know little about how or why this phenomenon works. The kingdom’s enemies fear certain frequencies because the vibrations torment them and leave them vulnerable to attack.

After the early losses of colonization, several strongholds became isolated and had to seal themselves off and learn to survive on their own. This was when bells became essential to their defenses.

At dawn, the bell rings once to drive away any enemies waiting for people to emerge from the stronghold.

Before nightfall, the first bell signals that it is time to begin closing the gates. The Last Bell rings as the final entrances are sealed for the night. It drives off smaller creatures and other enemies that might interfere before the stronghold is secure.

Some creatures have developed mutations that leave them completely deaf. The bells’ vibrations still affect them. Hunger or the scent of blood can draw these creatures dangerously close to a stronghold in search of prey.

Travel between outposts and strongholds follows strict daytime schedules so that people and supplies reach their destinations before nightfall. Traveling after dark is difficult and costly, though emergencies sometimes make it unavoidable.

# Trial of the Bell
