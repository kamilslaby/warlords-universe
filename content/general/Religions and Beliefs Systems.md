# Religions and Beliefs Systems

## Human Zeal 

Based on the belies of [[Keepers of Hope]]

## Hive Believers

Depending on what type of hive it differs.

## Technocrats

Based on worldview of [[Technocrats]] - They belive in keeping the human spirit pure but extending it with the scicence and building the machines.

## "Blood"

Based on the teaching of the faction [[Order of Bloodmothers]]. 

## VU believers

It's hard to summarize this belief, since it diffrers a lot based on the region.
They belive that VU powers (or magic) is the manifestion of the God.

## Naturalists

They belvie in pure blood and not modifying the body. Only natural, old way of living. But in the same time. They reject the teaching of the [[Keepers of Hope]]. They often worship local Gods of [[Momei]] or [[Somor]].

## Believers of the Old God 

People that still believe the Old God, that has no known name. They belive in the right time he will come and reveal himself.
