## Culture

- Society operates at the stronghold level, where the Lord of each stronghold, together with a council (or senate) of representatives, governs the people.
- The Church of Zeal guides people along the righteous path and takes measures to prevent heresy. Heresy is defined as anything that challenges the belief in humanity as the greatest race. The use of VU magic and AI sentinel machines is also strictly forbidden.
- If the Church's peaceful and psychological efforts are deemed unsuccessful, the case is handed over to the Inquisition, which serves as the spiritual police.
- Capital punishment involves being sent outside the stronghold during the night. This punishment is known as the [[Trial of the Bell]]. The condemned are exiled with nothing but a small bell attached to a chain around their neck to repel creatures. If they survive long enough to hear the next bell the next morning, they are considered redeemed.
