# Pirates and Smugglers 

Nazwa wymienna, zlaeżnie od kontekstu i usług jakie preferują. Nie są centralnie zoorganizowaną frakcją. 

It's a minor faction. Depends on the region, but they usually work as a single big ship group with crew and hired soldiers to protect it. They do some illegal business, necessary evil. 

Even the [[Caravan Guild]] use their services for their own gain. They are being prosecuted by the [[Kingdom of Zeal (General Faction)]] but on the far regions of the planet they are hard to reach. They support [[Confederation (General Faction)]] in their opposition to the Kingdom.
