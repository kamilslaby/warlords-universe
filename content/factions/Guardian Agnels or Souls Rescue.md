# Name to be decied yet

(Guardian Angels sound corny. I thinking about "Soul Rescues" or "Reapers")

Neutral brotherhood of those called to save souls. 

It was formed by [[Salvador The Hand Of God]], the former Inquisitor, when his own daughter [[Laura]] was lost and never made back to home stronghold before the night. When the last bell rang he refused to give up on her and went outside walls to find her. 

He used his combined military training, experience from serving as the great prosecutor with the outlawed power of VU to search for his daughter. 

He never found her, but dedicated his life to support similar situations. Now the Guardian Angles are widely know, but not fully supported by [[Keepers of Hope]]. They are considered something like nessesary lesser evil. And even in some occasions they receive the Chruch blessings for their hunts. 

The problem with dying to [[Spirit Walker]] is your soul is being captured and your are enslaved for eternity. 

So Guardian angels free the souls or capture them if possible - meaning the souls has not been completely corrupted. If so they use prism molded from the sacred vulcanic ash to contain the soul and bring it home. 

Later it cam be placed into machine body or grown into organic host body. Both operations are risky and not guaranteed to be successful. 

The hunt can only be done during the Full [[Spirit Moon]]
