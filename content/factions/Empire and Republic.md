# Empire and Republic

Empire was formed as a form of global superpower taking roots from Poland combined with their most powerful allies. 

Republic was the counter power formed against former senate members of the empire few powerful families and global corporations that were against AI regulations. Those last played the most crucial part in it created sentinel AI that were powered by quantum computer powered by computation speed of black amber. 

It quickly took over the power ocer republic and was named "Machine God". 

Empire started war against it to save the humanity. It it now known as "The First AGI War". Republic won.

In the last act of desperate measures they lunched protocol "The Last Rain". The series of nuclear detonations that will obliterate whole earth. 

It was sacrificial effort to set free and save last piece of humanity that was on the board of mothership. The space ship destined to reach [[Prime-1]] planet. 

It succeeded. During that the "Gates Of Tomorrow" were also destroyed. 

Now the earth is radioactive wasteland. 

The Machine God was defeated and his computer wad taken abroad for computation purposes. Little to that they know it also emits the black amber VU cry for help for any sentinel AGIs. Therefore it will be a source of the problems for future generations.

The descendants of the Empire formed the [[Kingdom of Zeal (General Faction)]].
