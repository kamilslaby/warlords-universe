# Confederation

subfactions:
1. [[Basilisk Group]]
2. [[Harvesters|Harvesters]]
3. [[Order of Bloodmothers]]


Not everyone shares the values of Kingdom of Hope. 

The group of outposts and few strongholds have declared independence. They formed the confederation - that supposed to have democratic governance. 

Many internal power games are causing it to be inefficient and still dependent economically on the Kingdom. Kingdom need it's old outposts to function. 

Depending on the outpost or stronghold it being ruled by different group. Most of them by the mix of democratic group of representatives. Some of them are under the rule of [[Order of Bloodmothers]] some of by [[Basilisk Group]], some of by [[Harvesters]]
