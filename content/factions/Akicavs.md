# Akicavs 

It's both faction and race. 

The crab beings.

(etymologia: Aki-cavs = Końcówką ze skorupiAKI + poczatek od CAVerns + s jako liczba mnoga)

Semi-intelligent crab like creatues from swamps and beaches. Most of them live in the area of black beaches. We are not sure how inteligent they are, since they do not use the common lang. 

Also, depending on the place where thye live the might differ a bit in apperance:
1. caves
2. ashen
3. swamps.
