# Hive of Jaders

Both race and a faction of Jaders. 

Live Underground, they look like kreto jaszczurki. 

They chew on jade that due to their unique stomach bacteria is being converted to processed jade in a form of semi solid poop. 

That is being used in medicine. It helps heals the wounds, the aroma of it repells the [[Djins]] and [[Somor]]

Their meat and blood is very desirable.
