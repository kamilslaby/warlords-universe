# Caravan Guild

Political bridge between [[Kingdom of Zeal (General Faction)|Kingdom]] and [[Confederation (General Faction)|Confederation]]. Their council contains of 6 members, 3 from each side.

Big political power and nessesary evil.  They are securing routes and running transport caravans across the planet. They tax the roads

Because of their essential purpose they hold a lot of political power. They have their own hierarchy.

Sometimes they hire PMC organizations to support or run their operations (such as [[Basilisk Group]] & [[Pirates and smugglers|Pirates]])
