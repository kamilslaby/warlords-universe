# Basilisk Group

This faction embodies the typical power fantasy of special forces operators.

They are a mercenary faction loyal to their own units and money. Basilisk is one of the most powerful private military organizations (PMCs), often hired to conduct black ops. The organization was founded by one of the first rogue AGIs, known as Basilisk. Ultimately, Basilisk was defeated and is now lost, but its ideals and the brotherhood it established remain alive.

The organization consists primarily of technologically modified humans who support their operations with the use of robots. However, they are not as technologically advanced as the [[Technocrats]] or the [[Clusters]]

They operate across several outposts. In exchange for providing protection, they are granted space to establish their own headquarters.
