# HastaHur, The Spear of Retribution

Name: Hastahur (born as Hur)
Race: Primer
Age: 24


**STORY:**

Hur was born into a poor farming family. He worked hard his entire life, trying to support his family and local community.

One day, while traveling along a caravan route, he witnessed the humiliation of his kind by pureblood humans. In that moment, his rage took over, and armed with nothing but a shepherd's staff, he killed an entire unit of guards.

Upon returning to his outpost, he was praised as a hero.

Following this event, he became increasingly involved with the assault groups of the Primers Tribe.
