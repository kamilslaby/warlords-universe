# Dante, The Key Holder


AGE: 35+
Race: Human
Class: ? 
Abilities: VU
Family: Mother, Father (unknown)


STORY: 

Dante was born and raised in a middle class family in Uno Arx. He started as an apprentice in the local church library. His hard work was quickly recognized, and he was sent to a boarding school at a remote church monastery.

Later, he became a trusted scribe and consultant to the Great Inquisitor. He was offered a career in the Inquisition or politics, but he chose to continue pursuing knowledge of the world.

Throughout this time, he tried to support his mother, who was his only family. His father's whereabouts were unknown. The only thing Dante knew was that his father had supposedly died in a mining accident.

He was the firm belivers of the old king. 

His naive love for the truth earned him many enemies within the Church's ranks. In retaliation, certain voices in the dark suggested sending him to research prisms in an extremely dangerous region of the continent.

His mission failed when he was attacked by (REDACTED). His body was never found. He went missing.
