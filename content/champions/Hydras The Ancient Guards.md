# Hydras, The Ancient Guards

There is more than one Hydra. They are ancient AGI agents merged with mech and flesh. Depending on which one we are talking about. (moze wyrzuce ten temat AGI). They are connected in conciseness via VU realm. If one has died the other ones feel it and gain power based on it.
