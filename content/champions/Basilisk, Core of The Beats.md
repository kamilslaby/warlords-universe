# Basilisk, Core of The Beats


It is an AI embodimenet in multiple entities simultaneously. To destroy it permanently, all of its copies must be destroyed, along with its main server and all backups. Otherwise, it will be reborn with newly acquired knowledge, including how to kill you.

Komunikuje sie ze swoimi kopiami za pomoca transmiji po świecie duchowym VU. Dlatego jest odporny na EMP.
