# Somor

(so-mor = so from "sowa", owl. and mor from "morderca")

The race of owl people. 

They are a native [[Prime-1]] race of intelligent beings. They have eyes like those of owls and beaks, but have no wings. Their vision and hearing are superior to those of humans.

They love fresh human meat, and caravans are afraid of encountering them. They rarely venture outside the forest. Neither the Fog nor the VU is hostile toward them.

They can flip their eyes to activate their "Spirit Eyes." They have some knowledge of the Common Language and are capable of building their own societies.
