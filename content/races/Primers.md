# Primers

First people, hatched from colony eggs. Similar to humans, but smaller, fater and their skin is more grey and white. They are less intelligent then humans but have higher survival on the planet. 

Due to mutations from exposure to amber they sense vibrations better. 

They have been hatched without guidance by the machines. And after more then 4000 years of no connection with they makers they have been disconnected from humankind initial society. Now when the humans arrived there are frictions about it. But generally they stick with people in larger cities

The are few tribes that choosed independence.
