# Djins


Those are beings outside the limitations of a 3 dimensional world. They are manifestation of VU power. Depending on the faction we can get a little pieces of information about them. 

General consensus of them is that we know very little of them

They appear as Djins, mirages or sometimes they posses hosts human or animals and use them for their own purposes.
