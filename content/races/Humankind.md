# Humankind

Subtypes of humans: 
1. Humans
2. Homo Sapiens - People as we used to know them from the original Old Terra (Regular Earth)
3. Homo Novus - Enhanced humans with genetic mutations or electronic augmentation.
4. Homo Deus - Elites of the world that have high intelligence due to selective breeding and genes modification. They also live longer and on rare occasions have spirits, senses and powers.
5. Homo Phantasma - Laboratory grown humans, no soul (debetable), most of them were optimized for physical labor or breed as organ donors for the imperium. They are without face, one eye and a connection limb for receiving food. 
6. [[Primers]] - Egg hatched humans send as colony efforts to [[Prime-1]] before the Extinction Event.
